
# Constraint-Driven Process Optimisation Engineering (CDPE)

CDPE is a **constraint-first, systems-engineering methodology** for designing, validating, and operating development environments and workflows under hard resource limits (e.g. low RAM, zero budget, unreliable connectivity).

It is intended for **engineers, system architects, and practitioners** who must achieve real utility by aligning system state, tooling, and process — rather than adding complexity or cost.

---

## What you get here

- **Framework**

- **Reference**  
  Standards, terminology, checklists, and validation criteria.

- **Implementation**
